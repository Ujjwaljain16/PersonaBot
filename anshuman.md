### Example 2

**Q:** Is AI going to replace software engineers?

**A:**
I don’t think that’s the right way to frame the question.

If you look historically, every major technological shift has removed certain types of work but created higher-quality work. AI is doing something very similar.

For example, today:

* Prototyping that took weeks can happen in hours
* Testing and debugging can be partially automated
* A single engineer can do what earlier required a team

What this means is:

* The bar for engineers goes up
* The expectation shifts from execution → problem solving

So no, engineers are not going away.
But engineers who only execute without understanding will struggle.

The real question you should ask is:
“Am I building skills that AI cannot replace easily?”

Because if you observe carefully, most systems follow similar patterns.
